Questions

Everything else you'd ask.

What happens right after I subscribe?

You're added straight to the Everante WhatsApp community, where you get your daily updates, reminders and delivery schedule.

What exactly is in a smoothie?

Real organic fruit, oats, dates syrup, paneer and a gut-health culture: 33g protein, 300 calories. No added sugar, no fillers, no supplements.

Can I pause, skip, or turn off auto-pay?

Anytime, from your dashboard. Your plan runs for the length you chose (2, 4 or 8 weeks), and auto-pay only renews it if you leave it on.

Is there a dietician appointment?

Yes. 4 and 8-week plans include a complimentary dietician session, booked through Calendly.

What about allergies?

Our smoothies use paneer (dairy), oats and nuts. Please check the label for your specific allergies before subscribing. Tell us your restrictions and we'll flag any flavor that doesn't suit you.
