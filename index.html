Five steps between a supplier and your door. We publish all of them.

    Sourced

    Named suppliers, audited quarterly. Whole fruit and nuts, never concentrate.

    Small batches through the night, hours before the first delivery leaves.

  3. 03

Whole fruit, nut milks, oats, and whey isolate, pea protein or casein. No added sugar, no fillers, no powders reconstituted from concentrate.

Every formulation is designed and signed off by a registered nutritionist before it reaches a single member. Full nutritional breakdown ships on the bottle.

04 — Questions

Everything else you'd ask.

When does my delivery actually arrive?

Between 5:30 and 7:00 AM, in an insulated cold-chain bag. You choose the window; we collect yesterday's empties on the next pass.

What exactly is in a smoothie?

Real fruit, nut milks and oats, plus whey isolate, pea protein or casein — 33g protein, 300 calories. No added sugar, no fillers.

Can I pause, skip, or travel?

One tap in the app. Unused days roll forward as credit — nothing is ever lost.

How fresh are the smoothies?

Blended hours before delivery. If a bottle can't reach you within 48 hours, it doesn't ship.

What about allergies and preferences?

Set filters once — nuts, dairy, gluten or fully plant-based — and every flavor respects them permanently.
