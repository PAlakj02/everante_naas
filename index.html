05 — Questions

Everything else you'd ask.

When does my delivery actually arrive?

Between 7:30 and 8:30 AM, in an insulated bag to keep it cold.

What exactly is in a smoothie?

Real fruit, nuts, oats and dates with a vegetarian protein base — about 33g of protein per smoothie. No added sugar, no fillers.

Can I pause, skip, or travel?

One tap in the app. Unused days roll forward as credit — nothing is ever lost.

How fresh are the smoothies?

Blended fresh each morning for that day's delivery — never held overnight, never frozen for later.

Are Everante smoothies vegetarian?

Yes — every flavor is vegetarian. If you have a specific allergy, message us before you start and we'll tell you exactly what's in each blend.
