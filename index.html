08 — Questions

Everything else you'd ask.

When does my delivery actually arrive?

Before sunrise — our fleet completes drops between 5:30 and 7:00 AM. Your smoothies arrive in an insulated, cold-chain Everante bag placed silently at your door. You choose your preferred window in the app, and we collect yesterday's bag and empties on the next pass.

What exactly is in a smoothie?

Every bottle hits the same spec: 33 grams of complete protein and 300 calories. The base is real ingredients — fruit, nut milks, oats — plus whey isolate, pea protein or casein depending on the flavor. No added sugar, no fillers, no powder sachets to mix.

Can I pause, skip, or travel?

Yes — one tap in the app. Skip a day, pause a week, or switch on travel mode for longer trips. Unused days automatically roll forward as membership credit. Nothing is ever lost.

How fresh are the smoothies?

Blended overnight in small batches, a few hours before your delivery. If a bottle can't reach you within 48 hours of blending, it doesn't ship. Glass bottles are collected, sanitized and reused — nothing single-use.

What about allergies and preferences?

Set filters once — nuts, dairy, gluten, or fully plant-based — and every flavor you receive respects them permanently. Every flavor exists in both whey and plant-protein versions, always at the same 33g spec.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, in the app, in under a minute. It's a membership, not a contract — cancellation takes effect at your next cycle with no fees and no retention calls. Most members don't: 96% renew after month three.
